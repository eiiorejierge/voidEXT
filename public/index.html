The ritual / 03 steps

Set it once.
Run it anywhere.

Scale XT is a bookmarklet: a compact web app launched from your bookmarks bar. Setup takes under a minute.

  1. 01

    Reveal your bookmarks bar

    Press Ctrl + Shift + B on Windows, or ⌘ + Shift + B on Mac.

  2. 02

    Drag Scale XT up

    Drag the orange install button into the bookmarks bar. That is the entire installation.

  3. 03

    Open it on any page

    Click the Scale XT bookmark, sign in, and generate your first destination.
