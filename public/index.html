Setup

How it works

Scale XT runs as a bookmark. Setup takes less than a minute.

  1. 01

    Reveal your bookmarks bar

    Press Ctrl + Shift + B on Windows, or ⌘ + Shift + B on Mac.

  2. 02

    Add Scale XT

    Drag the Add Scale XT button into the bookmarks bar.

  3. 03

    Open it on any page

    Click the Scale XT bookmark, sign in, and generate your first destination.

Install

Add it to your bookmarks

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar. If dragging isn’t available on your device, copy the code and paste it into a new bookmark’s URL field.
